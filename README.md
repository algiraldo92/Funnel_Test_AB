### Funnel_Test_AB

#### Problem Statement:
As an analyst, you must investigate user behavior in the company's application. The users are divided into three groups: two control groups receiving the old fonts and one test group receiving the new ones. Your objective is to determine which font set produces better results.

---

#### Objective:
First, study the sales funnel. Analyze how users reach the purchase stage. How many users actually reach this stage? How many get stuck in previous stages? Which specific stages?

Review the results of an A/A/B test. The design team would like to change the fonts across the entire application, but management fears users might find the new design intimidating. For this reason, they decided to base their decision on the results of an A/A/B test.

---

#### Technologies used:

* Pandas
* Numpy
* Matplotlib (pyplot)
* Scipy (stats)
* Seaborn
* Math

---

### Results: 

In the project, when conducting the A/A/B experiment, we observed that there were no significant differences in the group distributions and that changing the fonts in the application did not produce relevant variations.

Through the conversion funnel analysis, it was concluded that the tutorial was not essential at any step and that users did not follow a specific order when interacting with the application.

The percentage of customers who complete the process (reaching "PaymentScreenSuccessful") or make a purchase is approximately 48%. The highest customer drop-off occurs when users leave after interacting with the main page.
